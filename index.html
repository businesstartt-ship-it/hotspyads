Spice Storage Guide

Keep your spices fresh, bright and fragrant

  • Close the lid tightly after every use so air and moisture stay out.
  • Store in a cool, dry place, away from the stove, oven, sunlight and the sink.
  • Always use a dry spoon. A wet spoon or steam from a hot pot can make spices lump and spoil.
  • Do not shake the jar over a steaming pot. Take out what you need first, then add it.
  • No need for the fridge. Damp air inside can make spices clump.
  • Use within a few months of opening for the best aroma and colour. Natural spices slowly lose strength over time.
  • Handle the glass jar with care and keep it away from the edge of a shelf and out of reach of small children.
  • Check before you cook. If you see moisture, mould or a musty smell, do not use it and message us on WhatsApp.
